Does Aishwaryam Gold buy gold from customers?

Yes. Aishwaryam Gold buys gold from customers, including old and used gold jewellery. Contact the team to discuss the items you want to sell and arrange an evaluation.

How is my gold’s selling value calculated?

Your offer depends on the assessed purity, eligible gold weight and applicable buying rate. Stones and other non-gold components can affect the payable weight. Ask for a clear explanation of the final amount and any deductions before accepting.

Can you help with gold pledged at a bank?

Aishwaryam Gold offers assistance with releasing pledged gold from banks and financial institutions for sale. Contact the team with your lender details to discuss the process, required documents and whether your jewellery is eligible.

What should I bring when I visit?

Call the branch before travelling to confirm the documents required for your sale. Ask about identity and ownership verification, any purchase bill you have, and lender documents if the gold is pledged.

Can I get the exact price online?

The team can discuss the current buying rate by phone or WhatsApp. An exact offer requires an assessment of your jewellery, including its purity and eligible weight. A quoted rate per gram is not a guaranteed final payout.

How do I receive payment?

Confirm the available payment methods and timing with the branch before accepting the offer. Payment follows your agreement to sell and completion of the required verification.
